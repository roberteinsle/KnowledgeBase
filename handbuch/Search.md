# Search

Full-text search finds pages and attachments in all spaces you may read.

- **Open:** search field in the header, key <kbd>/</kbd>, on a phone the **Search** tab in the bottom bar.
- **Only one space:** **Search** in the left sidebar searches only the current space; **Search all spaces** removes the restriction.
- **Search terms:** all words must occur. Beginnings of words are enough: `holiday` also finds "holidays".
- **Ranking:** hits in the title come before hits in the text.
- **Attachments:** found by file name; text, Markdown and CSV files also by their content.
- Hits are highlighted in the excerpt.

The search is language-neutral (no stemming), because content can be written in any of the five languages. Upper and lower case do not matter. Pages in the trash do not appear.
